why topic modeling
- unsupervised: works on the texts without the operator assigning categories. Useful when working with a large collection of unknown things.
- modeling of collocations based on the document (in this case the page): focus on the documents, rather than the words; also provides flexibility when working with problematic texts.
- provides more general summary of the documents by using percentage of words that tend to occur together.


For this project, I chose to use the MALLET implementation of LDA. 
- see what is possible with a generic modeling of topics
- the original implementation is agnostic with regard to time. 
- comparison to results from correlated topic models or from multiple corpora models may prove instructive.
