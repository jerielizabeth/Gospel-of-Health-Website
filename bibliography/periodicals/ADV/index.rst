.. | **Dates**: 1898-1905
.. | **Primary Location(s)**: Battle Creek, MI; Berrien Springs, MI
.. | **Titles**: *Training School Advocate*; *Sabbath School Worker*; *Advocate of Christian Education*

.. The *Training School Advocate* was a publication of the Battle Creek College and later the Emmanuel Missionary College. The publication advertised and reported on the activities of the college, as well as presented an argument for education that focused on training for Christian service.
